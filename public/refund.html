Refund Policy

Last updated: 30 April 2026

30-day money-back guarantee.
Try it. If it doesn't save you time, email us within 30 days and we'll refund the full ₹9,999. No interrogation. No "we just need to understand why". Just a refund.

1. When you can request a refund

You can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase date for any reason, including:

  • The plugin doesn't work for your use case
  • Technical issues we couldn't resolve
  • You changed your mind
  • You found a better tool
  • Personal financial reasons
  • You'd just rather have your money back

2. How to request a refund

Email hello@gstinvoice.app with:

  • Subject: "Refund request"
  • Your order email (the one you used at checkout)
  • Your licence key (the GST-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX shown on your success page or in the email)
  • Optional: a one-line reason — helps us improve the product, but you don't have to share

That's it. No forms, no questionnaires.

3. How long does the refund take?

  • We acknowledge your request within 24 hours (often faster during India business hours)
  • We initiate the refund through Razorpay within 1 business day of acknowledgment
  • Razorpay processes the refund to your original payment method in 5-7 business days (sometimes faster for UPI)
  • Total worst-case time from request to money in your account: ~10 days

4. What gets refunded?

Full ₹9,999 — minus any payment gateway fees that Razorpay does not refund (typically <₹100). For most refunds processed within 24 hours of payment, Razorpay waives all fees and you get the entire ₹9,999 back.

5. After the refund

Once your refund is processed:

  • Your licence key is deactivated
  • You're asked to stop using the plugin and delete your downloaded copy
  • You can re-purchase later at the then-current price (which may be higher; we don't restore the original price)

6. Edge cases

After 30 days: Refunds are at our discretion. If you genuinely have an unresolved issue, email us — we'll usually do the right thing. But the 30-day window is our guarantee.

Partial refunds: We don't do partial refunds. It's all or nothing.

Chargebacks: Please email us first before raising a chargeback with your bank. We'd rather refund you directly. Chargebacks cost both of us money and time.

Multiple licence purchases: Each licence is refundable separately within its own 30-day window.

7. We won't ghost you

If you email us about a refund and don't hear back within 48 hours, please email again with "URGENT" in the subject. We may have missed it. We don't ignore refund requests — that's not how we run things.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@gstinvoice.app.
