FAQ

Is this an alternative to Tally / Zoho Books?

For invoicing — yes. For full ERP/accounts/inventory/payroll — no. We replace Tally for the 60% of businesses that only need to send invoices and file GSTR-1.

Are these invoices legally valid?

Yes. Every invoice carries the 16 mandatory fields from Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017 — including GSTIN, HSN, place of supply, sequential invoice number per FY, and amount in words.

Do I need Claude Pro to use this?

No. The plugin works on the Free tier of Claude too. You'll just hit the daily message cap faster on heavy days.

What about e-invoicing on the IRP portal?

The Pro tier includes IRP push for businesses with turnover above ₹5 crore. The Starter tier outputs a clean PDF — manually paste UDIN/IRN if/when you cross the threshold.
